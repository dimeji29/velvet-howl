About Velvet Howl

Hey, I'm Dimeji

Every character has a story. Let's bring yours to life.

I'm the creator behind VELVET HOWL — a place where imagination meets character design and creativity takes on a personality of its own. I make custom fursuits, fursona designs, and avatars for VRChat and VTubing, and every project begins with your vision.

I believe your character should feel like more than a design. From the smallest details of a fursona to the distinctive features that make a character instantly recognizable, it should reflect your imagination, your style, and the personality you want to share with the world.

No two characters are exactly alike, and that's what makes this creative journey so exciting. Whether you're exploring a new character concept, commissioning a fursuit, or bringing a virtual persona to life, I'm here to help you explore the possibilities, develop your ideas, and create something that feels personal to you — something you can see, share, and connect with.

Let's create something unforgettable. Tell me your idea — I'd love to take the first step with you.

Your character. Your imagination. Your story. — VELVET HOWL
